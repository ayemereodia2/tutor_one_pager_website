A tutor made to show, not just tell

Learning clicks when you can see the idea.

More than answers on a screen. Tutor AI combines visual illustrations, a teaching companion, and a way to explore questions as they come up.

Concepts you can see

From place value to angles and equations, diagrams and animations make abstract maths easier to follow.

Curiosity is part of the lesson

A learner can ask a follow-up instead of waiting for the next slide. The visual explanation adapts to the question.

A friendly teaching presence

A visual tutor points, guides attention, and helps learners follow the board from one step to the next.

The question changes the explanation

When students ask “why?”, the lesson doesn't stop.

Students rarely learn in a perfectly straight line. Tutor AI is designed for the moment a learner needs another angle, a simpler step, or a reminder of something learned before.

  • ✓Answers grounded in the current lesson and board.
  • ✓Step-by-step illustrations—not just text replies.
  • ✓Helpful connections to prerequisite concepts when needed.
